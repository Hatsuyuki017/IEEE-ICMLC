We established a strict CPTAC$\rightarrow$TCGA GBM benchmark that links bulk RNA, methylation, and scRNA-derived marker programs under a single reproducible workflow. The strongest current external models were the compact transformer for 1-year survival and LASSO logistic regression for 3-year survival, while the prior-guided kernel transformer remained competitive only at the longer horizon. The ablation study showed that methylation is presently the dominant added modality, whereas the current six scRNA-derived programs contribute mainly interpretability rather than a large predictive gain. Taken together, the study contributes a realistic external benchmark, a biologically grounded prior-transfer mechanism, and a clear roadmap for improvement: harmonized methylation across cohorts, richer clinical covariates, and larger single-cell priors are needed before prior-guided multimodal transformers can claim clear predictive superiority in GBM survival modeling.
