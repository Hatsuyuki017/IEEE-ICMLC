All analyses were conducted from the user-designated local GBM resources, with the primary data stored under \texttt{/Volumes/HIKSEMI/GBM\_data\_v2} and mirrored into the dedicated project workspace \texttt{Academia/GBM/research}. From these assets we constructed a case-level multimodal manifest that joins the CPTAC-3 clinical master table, local bulk RNA-seq files, DNA methylation arrays, and single-cell outputs.

The final CPTAC manifest contains 196 clinically annotated GBM cases, including 195 cases with bulk RNA-seq, 193 cases with methylation arrays, 192 cases with matched RNA+methylation, and 17 cases with single-cell outputs. For strict external validation we retained the 195 CPTAC cases with RNA and clinical covariates as the training cohort, and we paired them with an external TCGA-GBM cohort of 286 patients containing harmonized RNA-seq and survival annotations. The CPTAC methylation branch was used in a separate internal ablation because comparable TCGA methylation features were not yet harmonized at the probe level.

The single-cell branch is represented by 17 \texttt{seurat.analysis.tsv} files and 17 \texttt{seurat.deg.tsv} files, together with sample-sheet references to raw and QC-filtered feature-barcode matrices. The Seurat analysis tables provide barcodes, quality metrics, cluster assignments, and embeddings, while the DEG tables provide cluster-level marker genes with adjusted significance and effect size. These resources support both the atlas-style single-cell figures and the transfer of scRNA-derived marker programs into the patient-level prognostic models.
